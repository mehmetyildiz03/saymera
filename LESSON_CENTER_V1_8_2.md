# SAYMERA Lesson Center — v1.8.2

Selecting an unlocked lesson in the Primary 2 Atlas now opens a Lesson Center instead of immediately launching questions.

## Channels

### Learn
Explicit teaching. Completed reference lessons can be reopened from the beginning without erasing mastery or rewriting their original completion timestamp.

### Practice
Practice is divided into content-specific sections from the v1.8 lesson contract. Sections open sequentially. Completed sections remain revisitable.

A native section has four base tasks. Completion requires:
- at least four attempts,
- at least three correct,
- the completion attempt itself must be correct.

If the base four do not satisfy the gate, up to two short recovery tasks are appended. A final wrong answer never completes the section.

Legacy evidence is shown as prior work but does not fabricate section completion.

### Review
Review is visible at all times but is only actionable when delayed retrieval is actually due. Scheduled review shows timing such as “Yarın” or “3 gün sonra”.

## Session behavior

Lesson Center sessions return to the Lesson Center instead of forcing a cooldown flow after every small section. Learn, Practice and Review sessions are explicitly scoped to the selected lesson; unrelated global review work is not prepended.
