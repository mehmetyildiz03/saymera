# P2 Compare & Order Reference Lesson

The Primary 2 `compareOrder1000` lesson follows the MOE learning experience for comparing place-value cards digit by digit from left to right.

Sequence:

1. Compare hundreds without relation symbols.
2. If hundreds are equal, compare tens.
3. If hundreds and tens are equal, compare ones.
4. Establish “the same as” when all three places match.
5. Introduce `<`, `>`, `=` only after verbal meanings are established.
6. Connect a spoken comparison sentence to its symbol.
7. Transfer pairwise comparison to ordering three numbers.

The guided representation task uses Turkish comparison language before symbol selection. Arithmetic operators such as `+` are not used as comparison distractors.

Base-ten visuals use exact geometry: a ten rod has 10 visible cells and a hundred square has a 10×10 grid.
